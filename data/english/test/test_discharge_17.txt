DISCHARGE SUMMARY

Sat-Jun-1970

Subject: Jacob Smith

The patient was admitted with a diagnosis of pyelonephritis. The admission was due to a presentation of fever, pain, urgency of micturition, and hematuria. The reason for hospitalization was the severity of these symptoms and the need for immediate medical intervention.

During the hospital course, the patient was provided with proper care, treatment, and services. This included administration of antibiotic therapy, monitoring of vital signs, hydration and pain management. Interventions also included renal ultrasound and urine cultures.

Laboratory results indicated a positive response to the treatment as evidenced by the decrease in inflammatory markers and the patient's self-reported reduction in pain and urgency of micturition.

The patient's condition upon discharge showed significant improvement. The fever had subsided and the patient reported less pain and urgency of micturition.

Information regarding the patient's condition and treatment plan was provided to the patient and the family. This included understanding the nature of pyelonephritis, the need for completing the antibiotic course, the importance of hydration and rest, and signs of relapse that should prompt immediate medical attention.

Provisions for follow-up care include an appointment with the consulting nephrologist, Cooper Wilson, within two weeks of discharge, or sooner if symptoms reoccur or do not continue to improve. The patient's medical record number 958 ygSdCz will be used for this appointment.

The patient was discharged with a prescription for a continuation of the current antibiotic treatment and pain management medication. Adherence to the prescribed medication and follow-up appointment were stressed as vital for complete recovery.

The discharge disposition is home with the understanding and agreement of the patient and family to seek immediate medical attention should there be any deterioration in the patient's condition or recurrence of symptoms.
