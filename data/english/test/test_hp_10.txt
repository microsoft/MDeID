# History and Physical (H&P) Note

### Chief Complaint and Reason for Visit

Patient, Oliver Thompson, presents with a chief complaint of paroxysmal dyspnea. Recurrent bouts of sudden, severe shortness of breath accompanied by orthopnea. The patient reports experiencing dyspnea on exertion, reducing physical activities.

### Past Medical History

No known history of cardiac or respiratory diseases. The patient denies previous similar episodes, smoking history, or environmental exposure to harmful substances.

### Vital Signs

Vital signs on examination are as follows:

- Temperature: 37.0 °C

- Blood pressure: 120/80 mmHg

- Heart rate: 85 beats/min

- Respiratory rate: 18 breaths/min

- Oxygen saturation: 96%

### Physical Exam

Physical examination reveals an alert and oriented Oliver Thompson. Respiratory examination reveals no abnormal breath sounds, no wheezing, and no rales. Cardiovascular examination reveals normal S1 and S2, no murmurs, rubs or gallops. 

### Review of Systems

- Cardiovascular: denies chest pain or palpitations

- Respiratory: admits to shortness of breath and dyspnea on exertion

- Gastrointestinal: denies nausea, vomiting or abdominal pain

- Musculoskeletal: denies muscle weakness or joint pain

- Neurological: denies headaches or dizziness

### Medications

Oliver Thompson is not currently on any prescription medication. No known drug allergies.

### Family History

No known family history of cardiac or respiratory diseases. 

### Social History

Oliver Thompson is a non-smoker, occasionally consumes alcohol, and denies use of illicit substances. The patient lives alone and works as a Pilot, airline.

### Results Section

Awaiting laboratory tests and imaging studies to further evaluate the cause of paroxysmal dyspnea. An ECG and chest x-ray have been ordered.

### Assessment and Plan

In view of the Oliver Thompson's symptoms, differential diagnosis includes paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea (PND), obstructive sleep apnea, and panic disorder. The patient will be further evaluated by Ryan Kingwith laboratory tests and imaging. The patient has been advised to avoid exertion until further evaluation. The current health situation of the patient will be re-evaluated on the basis of test results.
