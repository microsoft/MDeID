# History & Physical Note 

## Chief Complaint and Reason for Visit

Ava Martin presents with a central, severe abdominal pain radiating to the back, accompanied by vomiting and nausea characteristic of pancreatitis. Expresses discomfort and inability to perform daily tasks due to debilitating pain.

## Past Medical History

Ava Martin has a history of alcoholism but no other significant medical history such as diabetes or liver disease. No surgical history was reported.

## Review of Systems

Digestive: Reports recent episodes of abdominal discomfort, nausea, and vomiting. Denies constipation or diarrhea.

Cardiovascular: No chest pain, palpitations, or shortness of breath. 

Respiratory: No difficulty breathing, cough, or wheezing.

Musculoskeletal: Admits to back pain radiating from the abdomen.

## Physical Exam

Vitals: Blood pressure 120/80 mmHg, pulse 100 bpm, respiratory rate 20 breaths per minute, temperature 36.8°C. The patient's general appearance is ill and in pain.

Abdomen: Tender, especially in the upper epigastric region. No palpable masses. Bowel sounds normal.

## Allergies and Intolerances

Ava Martin reports no known drug allergies or intolerances.

## Family History

Ava Martin has a family history of gallbladder diseases but no significant hereditary disorders.

## Medications

Ava Martin is not on any chronic medications.

## Results

Laboratory results: 

- Serum amylase level significantly elevated (1000 U/L, normal range: 30-110 U/L)

- Lipase level also increased (800 U/L, normal range: 10-140 U/L)

- Complete blood count, liver function, and renal function tests normal.

## Assessment

Given symptoms and the elevated serum amylase and lipase level, the working diagnosis is Acute Pancreatitis. The cause appears to be excessive alcohol consumption with a potential contribution from the family history of gallbladder disease.

## Plan

Admission for pain control, hydration, and monitoring. Avoidance of alcohol and dietary modifications. Regular blood tests will be done to monitor the levels of amylase and lipase in the blood. Ava Martin will be followed up by Ruby Thomasdaily during the hospital stay.

## Instructions

Ava Martin is advised to stay well hydrated, rest, and avoid alcohol. Follow-up is needed post-discharge to monitor recovery and evaluate the need for further treatment.

## General Status

Ava Martin is stable, but in distress due to severe abdominal pain. Will remain under close observation.

## Social History
