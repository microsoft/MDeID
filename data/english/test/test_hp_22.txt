## Patient History & Physical Examination

**Chief Complaint and Reason for Visit:**

Isla Taylor visits with a chief complaint of persistent breakthrough pain, radiating pain in the back, and frequent bouts of shortness of breath. They expressed concern about these symptoms being related to their ongoing condition of sickle cell anemia.

**Vital Signs Section:**

Upon initial encounter, vital signs are as follows: Blood Pressure: 130/85 mmHg, Pulse: 110 bpm, Respiratory Rate: 18 breaths/min, Temperature: 36.7°C.

**Past Medical History:**

Isla Taylor has a chronic history of sickle cell anemia, a genetic disorder that has been managed with hydroxyurea therapy. There is no known history of other significant illnesses or hospitalizations.

**Medications Section:**

Currently, Isla Taylor is on a regimen of Hydroxyurea 500mg daily and Folic Acid 1mg daily. They are also prescribed tramadol as needed for breakthrough pain.

**Physical Exam Section:**

Upon examination, Isla Taylor appears fatigued and dyspneic. Cardiovascular examination is normal with no murmurs, rubs or gallops. Respiratory examination reveals decreased breath sounds bilaterally, more pronounced on the left.

**Allergies And Intolerances Section:**

No known allergies or intolerances.

**Family History Section:**

Family history reveals that both parents are carriers of the sickle cell trait. No other family history of hematological or other significant conditions.

**Social History Section:**

Isla Taylor is a non-smoker, occasional drinker. They maintain a balanced diet and participate in light physical activities as tolerated.

**Results Section:**

Laboratory tests show a decrease in hemoglobin levels, indicative of anemia, and a peripheral smear confirms the presence of sickled cells. Chest X-ray reveals a 'sickle-chest' syndrome, characterized by infiltrates and atelectasis.

**Review of Systems Section:**

Isla Taylor reports breakthrough pain, back pain, and shortness of breath as the primary symptoms. They also indicate occasional fatigue and weakness.

**Assessment and Plan:**

In collaboration with Alan Harrison, it was determined that Isla Taylor's current symptoms are indicative of a sickle cell crisis. Management will continue with pain control, hydration, and oxygen therapy if needed. Isla Taylor's Hydroxyurea dose will continue unchanged. Increased monitoring of symptoms and a follow-up in one week have been scheduled.

**Instructions Section:**

Isla Taylor is advised to drink plenty of fluids, avoid stress, cold temperatures, and hypoxia. They are also advised to take their medications as prescribed and to reach out to Alan Harrisonor visit the hospital's emergency department in case of severe pain or other worsening symptoms.
