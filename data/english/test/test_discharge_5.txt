# Discharge Summary

## Discharge Summary for Mia Wilson

### Admission Reason:

Mia Wilson was admitted due to severe abdominal discomfort which was later diagnosed as pancreatitis.

### Hospital Course:

During the hospital stay, Mia Wilson suffered from bouts of vomiting and persistent nausea. Their condition was stabilized and monitored under careful attention.

### Allergies and Intolerances Section:

Mia Wilson reported no known allergies or intolerances.

### Discharge Diagnosis:

Mia Wilson was diagnosed with pancreatitis and was treated accordingly.

### Procedures Performed:

An ultrasound was performed to confirm the diagnosis of pancreatitis. Further lab tests were also carried out to assist in treatment planning.

### Care, Treatment, and Services Provided:

Mia Wilson was administered appropriate medication for alleviating symptoms and treating the condition. This included analgesics for pain, antiemetics for nausea, and necessary fluids to combat dehydration due to vomiting. Mia Wilson's nutrition was also closely monitored.

### Condition at Discharge:

Mia Wilson's condition improved considerably, with a significant reduction in nausea and vomiting episodes. Mia Wilson reported a decrease in abdominal pain and showed positive signs of recovery.

### Information Provided to Patient and Family:

Both Mia Wilson and family were provided with comprehensive information regarding the condition, necessary lifestyle changes, and the importance of adhering to medication schedules.

### Provisions for Follow-up Care:

Mia Wilson is scheduled for multi-disciplinary follow-up appointments with their primary care provider and the gastroenterology team. This is to ensure the continuity of care, monitoring of the patient's condition and any required adjustments in the treatment plan.

### Plan of Treatment Section:

Mia Wilson is to continue with the prescribed medication regimen and maintain a low-fat, healthy diet to manage the condition. Regular exercise and avoidance of alcohol are strongly recommended.

### Hospital Consultations Section:

Consultations with a gastroenterologist, dietitian, and physical therapist were arranged for Mia Wilson during the stay.

### Results Section:

Results from the ultrasound and lab tests confirmed the diagnosis of pancreatitis.

The staff at Grand Sable Plantation Saint Vincent wishes Mia Wilson a swift recovery and good health.
