# Discharge Summary

## Patient: Judith Hernandez

## Hospital: Frederick William University

## Admission Diagnosis: 

The patient was admitted due to a severe case of gout, which was accompanied by symptoms such as hot flush, intense pain, redness, swelling and notable erythema. 

## Hospital Course: 

Throughout the course of the hospital stay, the patient was administered a course of treatment designed to alleviate the pain and subdue the inflammation caused by the gout. This included steroid injections, NSAIDs, and colchicine. 

## Procedures Performed:

A synovial fluid analysis was performed to confirm the diagnosis, which indeed, revealed urate crystals indicative of gout.

## Discharge Diagnosis: 

Upon discharge, the condition of the Judith Hernandez had significantly improved with the erythema and swelling greatly reduced. The patient reported a substantial decrease in pain.

## Plan of Treatment:

The Judith Hernandez was prescribed allopurinol, a medication used to prevent gout attacks. It was emphasized that this medication must not be used during a bout of gout, but instead to prevent future occurrences. 

## Discharge Medications:

Kelly Andrews prescribed allopurinol, to be taken regularly to prevent further gout flare-ups. 

## Hospital Consultations: 

The Judith Hernandez was referred to a rheumatologist, Dr. Kelly Andrews, for further care and management of the condition. 

## Information Provided to the Patient and Family:

The patient was educated about dietary modifications to manage gout, which included reducing intake of alcohol, sugary drinks and foods high in purines. It was also advised to maintain a healthy body weight and engage in regular exercise.

## Provisions for Follow-up Care:

A follow-up appointment with the referred rheumatologist, Dr. Kelly Andrews, has been scheduled within a month of discharge.
