Discharge Summary

Chief Complaint Section:

Elijah Bouchard was admitted due to a significant onset of shortness of breath, coupled with hypoxemia, tachypnea, and hemoptysis.

History of Present Illness Section:

Upon admission, the patient's condition was critical with unstable vitals. The patient exhibited severe shortness of breath, hypoxemia, tachypnea, and hemoptysis.

Admission Diagnosis Section:

The preliminary diagnosis upon admission was a possible pulmonary embolism.

Hospital Course Section:

During the course of the patient's stay at the Lincolnton, the patient was subjected to various diagnostic tests, including CT scans and D-Dimer test. The diagnosis of pulmonary embolism was confirmed. The patient was immediately put on anticoagulation therapy to prevent further clot formation and to help dissolve the existing clot. 

Procedures Section:

The primary procedure performed was the administration of anticoagulation therapy. The patient was closely monitored for improvements in symptoms as well as potential side effects of the medication.

Results Section:

Over the course of treatment, Elijah Bouchard's conditions have shown considerable improvement. The vitals have stabilized with an enhanced oxygen saturation level, the shortness of breath has decreased, and there is a decline in hemoptysis and tachypnea.

Discharge Diagnosis Section:

Upon discharge, the patient's primary diagnosis is pulmonary embolism.

Plan of Treatment Section:

Post-discharge, Elijah Bouchard is advised to continue with the prescribed anticoagulation therapy under the supervision of Max Jones. Regular monitoring of the patient's vitals, especially oxygen saturation, is recommended.

Hospital Consultations Section:

During the hospital stay, consultations were done with a Pulmonologist for expert opinion and treatment plans. 

Discharge Medications Section:

Elijah Bouchard is to continue with the prescribed anticoagulation therapy. Regular monitoring of blood clotting times is recommended to adjust the dosages accordingly.

Hospital Discharge Instructions Section:
