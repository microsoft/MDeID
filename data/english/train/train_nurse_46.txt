"**Nurse Narrative Note: Heck's Disease Diagnosis and Treatment**

Subjective: 
Patient Haley Wong, a 35 male, initially presented with varied small swellings within his oral cavity. These were first noticed around the time of a high fever approximated to be three months prior. The swellings remained and grew in size even after the fever had abated. The patient did not have any significant medical, dental, or familial history.

Objective: 
An extraoral examination did not reveal any specific findings. Intraoral examination disclosed soft, sessile papules ranging from 2-10 cm in dimension present on both sides of the lower lip and the left ventral aspect of the tongue. Hematological parameters were within normal limits. Histopathological tests indicated benign parakeratotic hyperplastic mucosa with marked papillomatosis and acanthosis, and PCR analysis confirmed HPV subtype 32.

Assessment: 
The primary diagnosis is Heck's disease (focal epithelial hyperplasia), as confirmed by histopathological and PCR results showing HPV subtype 32. Differential diagnoses that were considered included squamous cell papilloma, condylomata acuminata, mucocele, and focal dermal hypoplasia (Goltz\u2013Gorlin syndrome).

Plan: 
The treatment involved the surgical removal of the lesions using a diode soft tissue laser under local anesthesia. The patient will be monitored for recurrence or the appearance of new lesions, with instructions for the parent to return for follow-up if new concerns arise.
