**Emma Murray: Discharge Summary and Treatment Plan**

**Chief Complaint Section** 

S3u0MJ91, complained of generalized pruritus, high fever, and appearance of red macule on the skin. 

**Hospital Course Section** 

The patient was admitted due to the aforementioned symptoms. Diagnostic procedures were carried out to identify the source of the symptoms. 

**Discharge Diagnosis Section** 

Following a thorough examination and subsequent tests, the patient was diagnosed with exanthema.

**Plan of Treatment Section**

The treatment plan involved the administration of antihistamines to alleviate the pruritus and antipyretics for the fever. A topical cream was prescribed to reduce redness and inflammation of the macule. 

**Hospital Consultations Section**

Consultations were made with a Dermatologist, and Infectious Disease Specialist. 

**Procedures Section**

Blood tests were conducted to rule out any underlying infections which could be responsible for the exanthema. 

**Results Section**

Blood tests returned normal, indicating that the exanthema was likely a result of an allergic reaction and not due to a viral infection. 

**Discharge Medications Section**

The patient is to continue the medications as prescribed during the course of treatment: antihistamines, antipyretics, and the topical cream. The patient was advised to avoid known allergens. 

**Allergies And Intolerances Section**

The patient has an intolerance to penicillin, which was taken into account during treatment. 

**Hospital Discharge Instructions Section**

The patient was instructed to complete the prescribed medication course and to contact Megan Austinor the clinic if symptoms persist or worsen. 

**Functional Status Section**

Upon discharge, the patient was able to perform daily activities independently. 

**Advance Directives Section**

The patient has an advanced directive in place. 

**Discharge Disposition**

Patient discharged to home under self-care with a follow up appointment with the clinic.
