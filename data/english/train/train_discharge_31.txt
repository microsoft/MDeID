Discharge Summary - Degenerative Polyarthritis

# Chief Complaint Section

Nathan Wratten presented with complaints of pain, intolerance to certain food items, and numbness in their hands. These symptoms led to the diagnosis of degenerative polyarthritis.

# History of Present Illness Section

Nathan Wratten was initially admitted due to an increasing severity of joint pain affecting their ability to perform daily activities. The associated symptom of food intolerance was atypical, however, further investigations revealed it to be a secondary effect of the underlying illness. The numbness of the hand was a more recent development and was bringing additional discomfort.

# Hospital Course Section

Over the course of their stay, the Nathan Wratten was subjected to several treatments and rehabilitation therapies. Pain management included the use of non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) and in severe instances, corticosteroids were administered. Physical and occupational therapy sessions were organized which focused on preserving joint mobility and functionality.

# Discharge Diagnosis Section

Nathan Wratten was diagnosed with degenerative polyarthritis. Alongside this, secondary symptoms of food intolerance and numbness in the hands were also noted.

# Plan of Treatment Section

On discharge, the Nathan Wratten is advised to continue with their pain management medications. They are also recommended to follow a structured diet plan, which seeks to mitigate the food intolerance issues. Regular physiotherapy sessions should also be maintained to preserve hand mobility and strength.

# Discharge Medications Section

Nathan Wratten is prescribed NSAIDs and corticosteroids. Additionally, daily doses of multivitamins for overall health and wellbeing are also suggested.

# Hospital Discharge Instructions Section

Post discharge, Nathan Wratten is advised to follow their prescribed medication regimen and to attend regular follow-ups with their primary care physician, Isabella Wilson. A dietary consultation will further help manage food intolerances. 

# Allergies And Intolerances Section

To date, Nathan Wratten has shown no known allergies to any medication. However, intolerance towards certain foods has been identified. Therefore, a detailed review of their dietary habits is recommended.

# Procedures Section

During the hospital stay, the Nathan Wratten underwent an MRI scan to evaluate joint degeneration. They were also subjected to nerve conduction studies to better understand the numbness in their hands.

# Results Section

The MRI scan revealed significant degenerative changes across multiple joints, a key feature of polyarthritis. Nerve conduction studies indicated mild peripheral neuropathy, possibly explaining the numbness in the hands.
